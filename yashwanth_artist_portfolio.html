A little about the artist

Creating with curiosity, feeling & detail.

I'm Yashwanth, an independent artist exploring different forms of visual expression. From canvas paintings and custom portraits to charcoal sketches, wall art and traditional work, I enjoy bringing ideas and personal stories to life through art.

Every piece is an opportunity to experiment, learn and create something meaningful. I also take on selected custom commissions, working with each client to understand the idea behind their artwork.
